Services

Ten corporate intelligence services, each with a defined scope, a lawful collection framework and deliverables you can rely on. Every service page follows the same fourteen-part structure, so you can compare them directly.

How to read this catalogue. Services can be commissioned individually or combined. Where an engagement draws on several services, the scope letter names each one. The ten-stage Intelligence Methodology Framework is not a service: it is how every service is delivered.

  1. 01

    Corporate Due Diligence & Ownership Intelligence

    A verified profile of a company's legal identity, registered officers, filing history and legal shareholding, built from official registers and public filings.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  2. 02

    Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) & Control Structure Mapping

    Ownership chains and control mechanisms traced toward the natural persons who ultimately own or control an entity, with every link graded for evidential strength.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  3. 03

    Enhanced AML / Sanctions & PEP Screening Intelligence

    Analyst-reviewed screening against official sanctions lists and PEP criteria, with every potential match resolved, explained and graded instead of left as a raw hit.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  4. 04

    Litigation & Regulatory Exposure Intelligence

    Review of published court decisions, insolvency records and regulator publications to assess a company's legal and supervisory track record.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  5. 05

    Reputational Risk & Adverse Media Intelligence

    Structured review of credible media and public sources for allegations and controversies, separating reported facts from claims and assessing their relevance.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  6. 06

    Strategic Partner & Vendor Risk Assessment

    A decision-ready risk assessment of a prospective partner, supplier or distributor, scoped to the specific relationship and its criticality.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  7. 07

    Digital Footprint & Executive Exposure Analysis

    A defensive assessment of what an organisation and its informed executives expose publicly online, and how that exposure could be used for fraud or targeting.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  8. 08

    Brand Protection & Intellectual Property Exposure Monitoring

    Scheduled review cycles of trademark registers, domains, marketplaces and social platforms to find infringements, impersonation and conflicting IP filings.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  9. 09

    Cross-Border Corporate Intelligence & Jurisdictional Risk Profiling

    Corporate intelligence across several jurisdictions, with a profile of each jurisdiction's disclosure regime, risk listings and the reliability of its records.

    Explore ServiceRequest
  10. 10

    Crisis Intelligence & Rapid Exposure Assessment

    A time-boxed open-source assessment when a crisis breaks: what is publicly known, what is being claimed, and what exposure follows for you.

    Explore ServiceRequest
