Ethical Commitment Statement

The commitments below apply to every engagement, every client and every source. They are part of our terms, not an aspiration.

  1. Lawfulness

    We collect and use information only where it is lawful to do so, under the GDPR, the Dutch AVG and all other applicable law, in every jurisdiction involved.

  2. Legitimate purpose

    We accept work only when it serves a legitimate business decision. We decline requests aimed at harassment, intimidation, discrimination, retaliation or personal curiosity.

  3. Proportionality

    We collect the minimum information needed to answer the agreed questions, about the minimum number of people, for the minimum time.

  4. No intrusion or deception

    We do not hack, scrape behind logins, use false identities, pretext, bribe, or buy leaked or stolen data. We do not contact the people we research.

  5. Fairness to subjects

    We respect the presumption of innocence. Allegations remain allegations in our reports, and we record denials, corrections and dismissals where they exist.

  6. Honesty with clients

    We tell clients what we found, what we did not find and how confident we are. We do not tailor findings to the outcome a client wants.

  7. Protection of vulnerable people

    We decline work targeting journalists, activists, whistleblowers or private individuals, and we exclude information about children.

  8. Confidentiality and security

    We protect client and subject information with appropriate technical and organisational measures and delete it when the retention period ends.

  9. Independence

    We disclose conflicts of interest and decline engagements where our objectivity could reasonably be questioned.

  10. Accountability

    We document our reasoning so that our work can be reviewed, and we correct errors promptly when they are identified.

If you believe OSINT AITTE has acted contrary to these commitments, contact intelligence@aite-rotterdam.com. If your concern relates to personal data, you also have the right to complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP) (opens in a new tab).
