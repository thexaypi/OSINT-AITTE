Engagement Process

Every engagement follows the same eight steps, from first enquiry to deletion of working files. Nothing is collected before scope, purpose and legal basis are agreed in writing.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You describe the decision you face, the entity involved and your timeline, through the contact page or by email. Please share only what is needed at this stage.

  2. 02

    Eligibility and conflict check

    We confirm that the request has a legitimate business purpose, that we have no conflict of interest, and that the work does not require permissions we do not hold.

  3. 03

    Scoping conversation

    We agree the questions, the service or services involved, the entities and persons in scope, jurisdictions, sources to be excluded and the delivery date.

  4. 04

    Proposal and engagement letter

    You receive a written scope, fee and timeline. The engagement letter records the purpose, legal basis, data protection roles, confidentiality, permitted use of the report and retention terms. Where we act as processor, a data processing agreement under Article 28 GDPR is included.

  5. 05

    Collection and analysis

    Work proceeds through the ten-stage methodology. If findings suggest the scope should change, we stop and agree any change with you in writing first.

  6. 06

    Quality review

    The report is checked for sourcing, classification, proportionality and clarity before release.

  7. 07

    Delivery and debrief

    The report and source register are delivered securely to the agreed recipients, followed by an optional call to walk through the findings and their limits.

  8. 08

    Retention and deletion

    Working files are kept only for the agreed retention period and then deleted. You can ask us to confirm deletion.

Requests we decline. Locating private individuals for personal reasons, research on employees or job applicants without a lawful basis, investigation of journalists, activists or whistleblowers, any request involving intrusion or pretexting, and any request where the stated purpose does not match the questions asked.

Start with an enquiry

A short description of the decision and the entity is enough to begin. We reply with whether and how we can help.
