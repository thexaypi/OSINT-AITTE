Four kinds of finding, never blended

Decision-makers are rarely let down by missing information alone. They are let down by claims that were reported as facts, and by guesses that read like conclusions. Every OSINT AITTE report keeps these apart.

Verified fact

Confirmed by an authoritative source for that type of fact, or by independent sources that agree.

Source-attributed

Reported by a named source and presented as that source's claim, not as established fact.

Analytical inference

A judgement drawn from the evidence, with the reasoning and alternatives stated.

Unresolved

A question the available lawful sources could not answer, recorded as a gap.

From information to recommendation

  1. Collected informationWhat a source says, recorded with its origin and access date.
  2. Verified factsInformation that has passed corroboration for its type.
  3. Analytical assessmentsWhat the facts and claims together suggest, with a confidence level.
  4. RecommendationsOptions for your decision, clearly marked as advice rather than findings.

What we will not do

Clear boundaries make findings usable. They also protect you: intelligence obtained unlawfully can create liability rather than reduce it.

Read the Ethical Commitment Statement
  • Access systems, accounts or data without authorisation.
  • Use pretexting, false identities or covert contact with subjects.
  • Acquire leaked, hacked or stolen data, or buy access to it.
  • Claim access to protected registers beyond lawful entitlement.
  • Profile individuals beyond what the agreed corporate question requires.
  • Present screening hits or allegations as legal determinations.
