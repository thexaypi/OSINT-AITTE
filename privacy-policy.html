Privacy Policy

How OSINT AITTE handles personal data of website visitors, clients and the people who appear in our research. Last updated 16 October 2026.

1. Who is responsible

OSINT AITTE, [Registered business address to be added], Rotterdam, the Netherlands, registered with the Kamer van Koophandel under number [KVK number to be added], is the controller for the processing described in this policy, unless an engagement letter states that we act as processor for a client. Contact: intelligence@aite-rotterdam.com.

2. Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or embedded third-party content. Fonts are hosted on the site itself, so viewing a page does not send your data to font providers.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the service, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive or use this data to identify visitors.

The contact form does not send data to a server. It prepares an email in your own email program, which you choose whether to send. We then process what you send as described in section 3.

3. Enquiries and clients

When you contact us or engage us, we process your name, organisation, role, contact details, and the information you provide about your request. Purposes: responding to your enquiry, checking eligibility and conflicts, preparing and performing the engagement, invoicing and meeting legal record-keeping obligations. Legal bases: taking steps to enter into or perform a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), legal obligations such as tax record-keeping (Article 6(1)(c)), and our legitimate interest in running the business (Article 6(1)(f)). Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within a reasonable period, normally no later than twelve months after the last contact. Financial records are kept as long as Dutch tax law requires.

4. People who appear in our research

Our work concerns companies, but company records and media reports include people, such as directors, shareholders and beneficial owners. If you are such a person, this section is for you.

What we process
Professional identity data (name, role, appointments), information from public registers, published court and regulator decisions, sanctions and public function information, and published media reports, limited to what is relevant to the agreed corporate question.
What we do not process
Special categories of personal data under Article 9 GDPR. Data about criminal convictions and offences only where a statutory ground under the Uitvoeringswet AVG applies. Information about children. Information from private accounts, leaked datasets or unlawful sources.
Sources
Official registers (such as the Handelsregister), official publications, published judgments, regulator registers, sanctions lists and credible media. We do not collect information directly from you.
Purpose and legal basis
Supporting a client's legitimate business decision, such as due diligence, compliance or risk management, on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), assessed and recorded per engagement.
Recipients
The client and the recipients named in the engagement letter, and service providers acting for us under data processing agreements. We do not sell personal data.
Transfers
Where research involves jurisdictions outside the European Economic Area and personal data must be transferred, we use the safeguards required by Chapter V GDPR.
Retention
Working files are kept for the period stated in the engagement letter and then deleted. A limited record of sources may be kept for a defined period to substantiate the report if it is challenged.

5. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and in particular the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21 GDPR). Send your request to intelligence@aite-rotterdam.com. We may need to verify your identity. We respond within one month, which may be extended where the law allows. Where a legal exemption applies, we explain this to the extent the law permits.

You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (AP) (opens in a new tab).

6. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including restricted access to working files and encrypted storage and transfer of reports. If a personal data breach occurs, we follow the notification rules of Articles 33 and 34 GDPR.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.

Relevant legislation: General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (opens in a new tab); Uitvoeringswet AVG (UAVG) (opens in a new tab).
